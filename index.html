About us

Built by a linguist, for language learners

Quor Lingua was founded by Kunal Kumar, a graduate of EFLU Hyderabad who specialised in Italian as his major and German as his minor. With over three years of teaching experience, he started Quor Lingua to make foreign-language learning practical, engaging and genuinely accessible — not just another exam-prep course.

Today he leads a dedicated team of language educators helping students prepare for study-abroad applications, international careers, exams and everyday travel, entirely through live online classes.
